FAQ

Common questions.

How long does delivery take?+
Your memory page is ready within 24hrs of receiving your files. Framed orders ship in 3–5 days and arrive in 6–8 business days in the USA.
How does the QR code work?+
Open your phone camera, point it at the QR on the frame, tap the link. Your memory page opens instantly — no app needed. Works on any smartphone.
How many photos and videos can I include?+
Unlimited photos. For videos, share your YouTube links (upload as unlisted) and we include them on your page.
Will the page be available forever?+
Yes. Your memory page is hosted indefinitely. The QR code on your frame will always work.
Do you ship worldwide?+
Yes — USA, UK, Canada, Australia and most of Europe. Always free. Digital orders are available worldwide instantly.
Can I update the page after delivery?+
Yes — contact us anytime to add photos, videos or update the letter. The QR code never changes so updates appear automatically.

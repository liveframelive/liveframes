Common questions

Everything you need to know

How long does it take to receive my order?
+
Your personalised memory page is handcrafted within 24 hours of receiving your files. For physical framed orders, the frame is printed and shipped within 3-5 business days, arriving in 6-8 business days in the USA. Digital orders are delivered instantly via email once your page is ready.
How does the QR code work?
+
Simply open your phone camera, point it at the QR code on the frame, and tap the link that appears. Your memory page opens instantly in any browser — no app download needed. Works on iPhone, Android, and any smartphone.
How many photos and videos can I include?
+
There is no limit to the number of photos. You can also include multiple YouTube video links — we recommend uploading your videos to YouTube as unlisted and sharing the link with us. The memory page is designed to beautifully display as many memories as you want.
Will the memory page be available forever?
+
Yes. Your memory page is hosted on our servers indefinitely. We designed MemoreLive to last — the page and all its content will remain accessible for as long as MemoreLive exists.
Do you ship worldwide?
+
Yes — we ship to the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and most of Europe. Shipping is always free and takes 6-8 business days to the USA. Digital orders are available worldwide instantly.
Can I update the memory page after delivery?
+
Yes — contact us anytime and we can update your memory page with new photos, videos or a new letter. The QR code on your frame never changes, so updates appear automatically next time it's scanned.

↓ iii

A note, left in the margin

To the two people who taught us what staying looks like —

We grew up thinking your patience was ordinary. It wasn't. We watched you choose each other on the loud days and the dull ones, and somewhere in there you taught the whole house how to love without making a show of it.

Fifty Septembers. Monsoons and power cuts and weddings and small Tuesday dinners. Thank you for every single one.

— your children, always

"A marriage is a long conversation that never quite finishes."
